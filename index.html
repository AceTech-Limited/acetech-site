Capabilities

What We Do

Our work covers the whole life of a software product, from the first scoping conversation through design, engineering, launch and the years of support that follow. Nothing is handed off to an agency. The people who design it are the people who maintain it.

◆

Product Strategy

We shape what the product actually is. Feature sets, user journeys, engagement ideas, and a roadmap that survives contact with real users rather than one that looks good in a deck.

◉

UX & Interface Design

Interfaces for people using a phone, often one handed, often on the move. Every tap is designed for speed and clarity, because in a real-time app hesitation caused by the interface is a real cost to the user.

▣

Application Development

Application logic, rules engines, state management and the hundred small edge cases that only show up once real users start pushing on a product in ways nobody planned for.

◎

Mobile Engineering

Unity 3D clients and native builds for iOS, Android and desktop, tuned to run well on the low and mid range devices most users actually own, not just the newest handset in the office.

⬡

Backend & Real-Time Systems

Low latency servers, session and user state, recovery after a dropped connection, data stores, notifications, and the admin tooling teams live inside every day.

◈

Support & Optimisation

Release management, monitoring, scheduling, performance tuning and feature iteration. The ongoing engineering that decides whether an app that launched in March is still working well in September.

✦

Analytics & Admin Tools

Dashboards with real visibility into how a product is used. What features people rely on, where they drop off, and which usage patterns are worth a closer look.

◇

Security & Reliability

Secure coding, abuse and anomaly monitoring, access controls, logging and audit trails. On a live product this is not a feature, it is the baseline for being trusted at all.

₹

Integrations & Compliance Support

We build and support the technical side of third party integrations, APIs and reporting, and work with our clients' own legal and finance advisers so the software fits the rules they operate under.

How We Build

Our Process

We build with a product mindset rather than a delivery mindset. Launch is the start of the interesting part, not the finish line, so the way we work is shaped around what happens after the first release.

01

Scope

Goals, audience, the market you are really competing in, and the systems the product needs underneath it. We get uncomfortably specific about what success looks like before anyone opens a design file.

02

Design

Flows, interaction design, product systems and architecture planned together rather than in separate rooms. We prototype the parts that carry risk and keep reworking them until they feel right in the hand.

03

Build

Full stack development with QA, automated regression, real device coverage and load testing well above expected peak. Releases go out in stages so a problem reaches a few users, never all of them.

04

Support

Live monitoring, analytics, tuning, updates and a roadmap that keeps moving. This is where most of a product's lifetime is spent and where most studios stop paying attention.
